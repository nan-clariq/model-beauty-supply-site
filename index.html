Our story

Twenty years, one fire, and a new start

We spent twenty years serving families in East New York. On September 12, 2025, a fire in the apartment above our store ended it in a night.

Seventy-four neighbours chipped in to help us start again, and here we are: same family, same work, new address on Jamaica Avenue. The shelves are full.

Thank you for not letting it end there.
